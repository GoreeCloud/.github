# GoreeCloud community conduct

GoreeCloud collaboration should be respectful, technically constructive, and safe for participants.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully, including during disagreement;
- critique ideas and implementations rather than attacking people;
- provide useful context and evidence where practical;
- respect privacy, security, attribution, licensing, and project boundaries;
- avoid unnecessary disruption, spam, or repeated off-topic activity;
- help keep issue, review, and discussion threads focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in GoreeCloud collaboration spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse;
- discriminatory or demeaning attacks;
- deliberate exposure of private or identifying information without authorization;
- publishing credentials, secrets, private user data, or sensitive vulnerability details;
- malicious disruption, impersonation, spam, or knowingly deceptive technical claims;
- retaliation against someone for raising a good-faith security, privacy, safety, or conduct concern.

## Scope

This baseline applies to public collaboration in GoreeCloud repositories, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions where no more specific local conduct policy applies.

## Reporting concerns

For sensitive conduct concerns, use the [official GoreeCloud contact page](https://www.goreecloud.com/contact/) rather than posting private details publicly.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

GoreeCloud may edit, hide, lock, remove, or restrict content or participation when necessary to protect participants, keep technical spaces usable, or address violations of these expectations. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and available context.

Repository-specific conduct rules may add more precise requirements.
